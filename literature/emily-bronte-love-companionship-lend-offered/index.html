작품 소개

이 기록의 출발점은 Emily Brontë가 쓴 『Wuthering Heights』다; Project Gutenberg eBook #768에 수록된 문장을 직접 확인하고 동반이라는 질문을 붙였다; 이 기록의 세 낱말은 ‘lend’ · ‘offered’ · ‘soon’다.

He is fond of reading, and he thinks of leaving soon to get married; so he offered, if I would lend him books out of the library, to do what I wished: but I preferred giving him my own, and that satisfied him better.

— Emily Brontë, Wuthering Heights, Project Gutenberg eBook #768, plain-text paragraph near line 8890
